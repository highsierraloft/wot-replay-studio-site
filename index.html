WoT Replay Studio
OverviewPrivacyTerms

Your replays, made into videos.

A personal Windows application for recording World of Tanks battles, editing shot highlights, and preparing videos for YouTube.

Replay Studio runs on your PC and opens a local dashboard in your browser. This website describes the application and its data practices.

Browse & record
Filter your replay collection, select battles, and queue full-battle recordings or highlights with adjustable time before and after each shot.
Prepare your video
Keep the original export and create smaller copies. Add battle results and an optional victory soundtrack. Make portrait Shorts with a full-frame or center-cropped view.
Upload when ready
Review your video, edit the generated title and description, choose the audience and visibility, and submit it to your connected YouTube channel.

You control each upload

YouTube is optional. Connecting a channel and creating a preview do not upload a video. Each upload starts from a finished export after you submit its upload form. Unlisted is the default; Public and Private are also available.

WoT Replay Studio uses YouTube API Services. Google handles sign-in and permission consent. The app requests permission to upload videos and read YouTube account information so it can display the destination channel.

About this project

This is a personal utility maintained by highsierraloft. A compatible World of Tanks client, OBS and FFmpeg are used for local video creation. Replay compatibility and event detection depend on the installed game version.

For support or privacy questions, use the project issue tracker. Keep credentials and private files out of public issues.
