Privacy policy

Effective 18 October 2026

WoT Replay Studio creates videos locally. Its optional YouTube connection sends only the video and upload details you choose to submit.

Data on your PC

The application reads replay files from your selected folder, including battle metadata such as tank, map, player name, shots, damage and results. It stores the replay library, settings, job records, local logs, results images and exported videos on your PC. Titles and descriptions are generated locally from battle metadata.

The dashboard uses browser storage for interface choices such as selected replays. The application has no advertising or analytics service and does not send replay files or recording data to the project maintainer.

Google and YouTube access

WoT Replay Studio uses YouTube API Services. Google handles account sign-in; the application does not receive or store your Google password. Google and YouTube process information under the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.

The app requests these permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): send a selected finished video and its title, description, audience and visibility to YouTube.
  • Read YouTube account information (youtube.readonly): identify and display your destination channel, including its channel ID and title.

The app stores the OAuth client configuration, access and refresh tokens, channel identity, and upload records in an encrypted file tied to your Windows account. Upload records include selected metadata, progress, resumable-transfer state and the returned video link. This supports reconnecting to Google, resuming interrupted transfers and avoiding duplicate uploads.

Connecting an account does not upload a video. Each upload requires your submission of its form. Your chosen visibility controls how YouTube makes the uploaded video available. Public videos may be seen by anyone; unlisted videos can be viewed by anyone with the link. YouTube may continue processing a video after the transfer completes.

Google user data is used for this connection and upload workflow. The application does not sell it, use it for advertising, or send it to an AI service. The maintainer has no hosted application server receiving these credentials or files.

Retention, deletion and revocation

Data remains in your local installation until you remove it. Settings → YouTube → Disconnect on this PC removes the locally saved channel tokens and channel identity. The client configuration and upload history remain so you can reconnect and recognise earlier attempts.

You can revoke Google access at any time from your Google Account connections. To remove all locally stored YouTube credentials and upload records, close Replay Studio and delete data/youtube/connection.bin inside its installation folder. Local replay-library data and exported files can be removed separately from their folders.

Disconnecting or deleting local data does not delete videos already on YouTube. Manage or delete those videos in YouTube Studio. Clearing your browser's data for the local dashboard removes its saved interface choices.

This public website

GitHub Pages hosts these information pages. They contain no application credentials, private replay files, analytics scripts or advertising. GitHub's processing of website traffic is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Questions and updates

The project maintainer is highsierraloft. For questions or complaints about these practices, open a project issue. Public issues should not contain credentials, tokens or private files. Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised effective date.
